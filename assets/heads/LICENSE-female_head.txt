female_head_scan.glb -- female head bust for SilkCrowd
======================================================

The mesh and its skin texture are derived from MakeHuman / MPFB.

  * Original: MakeHuman 2.0 female base mesh ("base" mesh, vertex groups, and the
    "Human.body" 2048x2048 skin atlas), exported to glTF with the MakeHuman Plugin
    For Blender (MPFB). Obtained from the CC0 avatar collection shipped with
    met4citizen/TalkingHead (avatars/mpfb.glb).
  * MakeHuman's assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
    MakeHuman: http://www.makehumancommunity.org/
    MPFB:       https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2

What this repository changed (see tools/prep_female.py for the exact steps):

  * everything below the neck was cut away, apart from a short bust and a capped neck;
  * the bust was uniformly scaled (x0.905) and translated into SilkCrowd's head space
    (crown at y = +0.128 m, midline at x = 0, face toward +z);
  * MakeHuman's flat-red eyeball placeholder was replaced: the two eyeball spheres were
    deleted and rebuilt as front-projected globes, and their atlas islands re-painted
    with a pupil, an iris and sclera;
  * the body atlas is otherwise kept byte-for-byte at its authored 2048x2048 resolution
    and with MakeHuman's own UV layout, so lips, brows and ears stay registered.

CC0 places no attribution requirement on the result; the credit above is given because
that is the decent thing to do.
